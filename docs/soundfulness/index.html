An immersive experience

A calm counterpoint to a busy life.

Soundfulness is available as an add-on for your group stay. The experience blends traditional meditative instruments, natural soundscapes and ambient compositions in the peaceful countryside setting.

Traditional instruments

Singing bowls, gongs and other traditional instruments create resonant tones designed to encourage deep relaxation.

Natural soundscapes

Bespoke soundscapes blend natural elements into an auditory journey that complements the setting.

Ambient compositions

Original ambient compositions use carefully curated frequencies to support mental clarity, emotional balance and calm.

For your people

Make the retreat memorable.

A private sound bath can work beautifully for team relaxation, creative inspiration or a distinctive shared group activity.
