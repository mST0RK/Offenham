Private Wood-Fired Hot Tub

Fits up to 8 people comfortably so your group can take turns soaking together. Light the log burner, let the water heat up naturally, and unwind beneath the stars.

Shared Central Firepit

The ultimate hangout spot for your group. Cook over the open flame using the provided grills, share giant meals under the stars, and swap stories around a roaring campfire.

Extra Firepits Welcome

Need more warmth or separate cooking stations for your 20+ crowd? You are welcome to bring your own extra firepits, provided they are raised on legs to protect our beautiful fields.

Music & Guitars Welcome

Because we have no immediate neighbours, your group can play music and sing around the fire up until 11:30 PM! Don't forget to pack a guitar for the ultimate acoustic campfire session.

Piping-Hot Shower

A dedicated outdoor shower block delivering instant, refreshing hot water for the site.

Eco-Friendly Composting Toilet

A clean, sustainable, entirely self-contained toilet facility, meticulously maintained for a guilt-free eco footprint.
